# Search strategy summary

The review used publisher/proceedings sites, repositories, exact-title and topic-focused public-web searches, backward reference checking, forward/citing-paper discovery where accessible, benchmark-centered follow-up, and competing-review follow-up.

Search concepts covered screenshot-to-code, UI-to-code, design-to-code, image-to-web, sketch-to-code, webpage reconstruction, visual/multimodal specification, executable web output, benchmark, metric, validity, reliability, human assessment, and human preference.

Candidate native-database queries were prepared for Scopus, Web of Science, ACM Digital Library, and IEEE Xplore, but a complete retained export/deduplication trail was not obtained. The manuscript therefore uses the term “systematic evidence synthesis” and explicitly discloses the search-accounting limitation.
